🔍 ENERGY INSIGHTS

Why Could Your Bill Be High?

EnergyScope checks the information entered by the user and identifies possible consumption factors.

01

Family Size

Larger households may use more electricity because several people use appliances throughout the day.

02

Air Conditioner

AC usage can become a major contributor to household electricity consumption.

03

Long Appliance Usage

Using appliances for longer periods can increase monthly electricity consumption.

04

Saving Opportunities

Reducing unnecessary appliance usage can help lower electricity consumption.

💡 SMART ENERGY TIPS

Simple Ways to Reduce Electricity Use

❄️

Use AC Wisely

Avoid unnecessary AC usage and keep doors and windows closed while the AC is running.

💡

Switch Off Lights

Turn off lights when leaving a room.

🔌

Reduce Standby Power

Switch off appliances completely when they are not required.

🧺

Use Full Washing Loads

Avoid running several small washing cycles when one full load is possible.

🧊

Maintain Your Fridge

Keep the refrigerator properly maintained and avoid opening it unnecessarily.

⚡

Think Before You Use

Small daily changes can reduce monthly electricity consumption.
